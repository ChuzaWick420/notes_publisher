# How to Write a Cover Letter

## Parts

- Header
- Greeting
- Opening Paragraph
- Body Paragraphs
- Strong Closing
- Professional sign off

### Header

- Your name
- Your city and state
- Your phone number
- Your email address
- Date
- Name of recipient
- Title of recipient
- Company name
- Company address

#### Example

Ellen Lu  
Phoenix, AZ  
304-555-0192  
ow+091238@indeedmail.com  
April 9, 2026  
Belinda Piccio  
Senior Editor  
Wavewood Institute  
Phoenix, AZ

### Greeting

- Dear \[first and last name\]  
- Dear \[position title\]
- Dear \[Department\] Hiring Team,
- Dear \[Company Name\] Recruiting Team,

> [!QUOTE] Indeed.com[^1]  
> Consider addressing a specific person or title rather than "To whom it may concern."

#### Examples

- Dear Hiring Manager,
- Dear Benjamin Sondergaard,

### Opening Paragraph

- Mention the role you are applying for
- Mention where you discovered the job
- Explain your interest in the role
- Show the company that you have researched them

#### Example

> [!QUOTE] Indeed.com[^1]  
> I’m excited to apply for the Graphic Designer position at Wavewood. I understand you’re currently adding several new product lines, and I believe my skills in video and animation can help you create a successful launch.
> 
> As a longtime fan of your products, I’m thrilled at the opportunity to bring my unique style and passion for beachwear to the company.

### Body Paragraphs

- Brief overview of background in relation to the position
- Suitable to position, mention
	- achievements
	- skills
	- specialties
- Focus on 1 or 2
- Details of your success
- Measurable impacts

#### Example

> [!QUOTE] Indeed.com[^1]  
> As the Director of Human Resources at Champion Solutions, I was a key senior leader responsible for improving the efficiency and performance of the company’s 540 employees.
> 
> Before that, I worked in human resources for Cold River Optical, where I developed an employee retention plan involving a wellness program, an internal training program and a promotions selection process. This led to a 50% reduction in employee turnover.

### Strong Closing

- Summarize a specific story that shows you are right for the role
- If you are changing careers then talk about transferrable skills

#### Example

> [!QUOTE] Indeed.com[^1]  
> Achieving ambitious marketing goals is always a top priority, and I act in the company’s best interests. I look for ways to improve and reach new clients through innovative campaigns, and I enjoy delivering marketing presentations to potential clients and focusing on an organization’s strengths.
> 
> At Beta Experts, my marketing skills helped the company reach new levels of success, including a 45% increase in customer engagement. I’m never satisfied with the current state of things, and I believe that a company should continually seek out ways to improve and reach new clients through innovative campaigns.

### Professional Sign off

- Paragraph summarizing why you are applying for the role and why you would be a great fit.
- Anticipation of employer's response about possible next steps.
- Sign off
	- Sincerely,
	- Best regards,
	- Kind regards,
- If sending a hard copy, leave space for signature
- Full Name

#### Example

> [!QUOTE] Indeed.com[^1]  
> Thank you for your time and consideration. I am eager to learn more about the sales position and Wavewood Institute. Growth is essential to my continued success, and I’m excited for the chance to be a part of Wavewood’s industry-leading team. My proven track record and Wavewood’s quality products are a winning combination for increasing the company’s market share.
> 
> Sincerely,
> 
> Jane Brooklyn

## Best Practices

Consider the following before writing

- What talents, skills or accomplishments would you like the company to know?
- How you learnt about the job opportunity
	- If it's a person, write their name and title
	- If it's a job board then write where and when you discovered it
- List any specific instructions noted in the job description
- What drew you to the company
	- Culture?
	- Brand?
- How are the reviews
- Do you agree with their mission statement and vision?

## Tips

- 1 page long
- Professional font[^2] such as
	- Arial
	- Helvetica
	- 10 - 12 points font size
- 1 inch margins
- left aligned
- single spacing
- Add blank line between paragraphs and each major section
- File name: `<FirstName>_<LastName>_CoverLetter.pdf`
- examples of your skills in action (demonstration)
- Proofread
	- Read 2-3 times
	- Read aloud to detect awkward phrases

## Ats Tips[^3]

Have dedicated labeled sections for 

- Experience
- Skills 
- Education

Avoid using

- Images
- Icons
- Tables
- Columns

File Formats

- Accepted
	- Word
	- PDF
- Avoid
	- JPEG
	- PNG

Tailoring resume

- Add skills listed in the job description to your skills, projects and work experience sections
- Quality of tailored resume matters more than number of jobs you have applied to
- Use a simplified resume format
